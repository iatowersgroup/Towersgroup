What we do
Engineering Solutions

From high-voltage substations to low-voltage installations, we cover the full spectrum of electrical engineering needs with specialized expertise at every level.

High & Medium Voltage Systems

Design and engineering of electrical infrastructure for the energy sector — from transmission lines to substations and industrial connection systems.

  • Design of electrical connection systems for new industrial facilities
  • Basic and detailed engineering of electrical lines and substations
  • Formulation and structuring of electrical projects
  • Loadability analyses, power flow & short-circuit evaluations
  • Protection coordination and adjustment studies (EACP)
  • Consulting in regulatory compliance, load capacity & system reliability

Low Voltage Systems

Complete electrical solutions for industrial, commercial, and residential applications — from design and renovation to emergency response and diagnostics.

  • Electrical designs for industrial, commercial & residential applications
  • Electrical enhancements and renovations in structures, educational facilities & industrial plants
  • Emergency services: transformer replacement, component repair & reconnections
  • Modernization of switchboards, grounding systems, conduits & lighting
  • Technical inspections and comprehensive electrical diagnostics

Consulting & Project Development

Expert guidance on energy project development, regulatory compliance, and technical feasibility — from concept to final approval with operators and regulatory bodies.

  • Comprehensive energy project formulation and development
  • Energy efficiency consulting and regulatory compliance guidance
  • User connection procedures and technical evaluations
  • Design and expansion feasibility assessments
  • Permit management, technical documentation preparation
  • Procedures and filings with operators and regulatory entities

Electrical Supplies & Technical Assistance

Integrated electrical supply management and hands-on technical support — from material procurement through assembly, testing, and commissioning.

  • Provision and management of domestic and imported electrical materials
  • Full compliance with project specifications, technical standards & quality requirements
  • Direct coordination with electric utilities and international partners
  • Dependable supplies customized to each project's specific needs
  • Technical support throughout assembly, testing & commissioning phases
