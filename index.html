What we do
Our Services

High & Medium Voltage Systems

Design and engineering of electrical infrastructure for the energy sector, from substations to transmission systems.

  • Design of electrical connection systems for new industrial facilities
  • Basic and detailed engineering of electrical lines and substations
  • Loadability analyses, power flow & short circuit evaluations
  • Protection coordination and adjustment (EACP)
  • Consulting in regulatory compliance and electrical reliability

Low Voltage Systems

Complete electrical solutions for industrial, commercial, and residential applications — design, renovation, and diagnostics.

  • Electrical designs for industrial, commercial & residential applications
  • Electrical enhancements and renovations across all facility types
  • Emergency services: transformer replacement, reconnections & repairs
  • Modernization of switchboards, grounding systems & conduits
  • Technical inspections and electrical diagnostics

Consulting & Project Development

Expert guidance on energy project development, regulatory compliance, and technical feasibility from concept to approval.

  • Energy efficiency consulting and regulatory compliance guidance
  • User connection procedures and technical evaluations
  • Design and expansion feasibility assessments
  • Permit management and technical documentation preparation
  • Procedures for operators and regulatory entities

Electrical Supplies & Technical Assistance

Integrated electrical supply management and hands-on technical support throughout your project lifecycle.

  • Integrated electrical supply and material management solutions
  • Full compliance with project specifications & technical standards
  • Direct coordination with electric utilities and international partners
  • Customized supplies tailored to each project's specific needs
  • Technical support through assembly, testing & commissioning
